Questions before you switch

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Frontdesk runs on the WhatsApp Business number your customers already have saved.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within a few working days of connecting their calendar and confirming their services and hours with us.

What happens if it can't answer something?

It's handed straight to you on WhatsApp with the context of the conversation, instead of leaving the client stuck in a loop.

Can it work alongside our current booking system?

In most cases, yes — Frontdesk connects to the calendar or booking tool you already run rather than replacing it. If your setup is unusual, that's exactly the kind of integration Rivance handles directly.

Is our client data kept private?

Conversations and bookings are stored to keep an accurate record for your business only, and are not shared with other businesses or used to train anything outside your account.
