What a missed WhatsApp actually costs

Not a hypothetical — this is what happens in most appointment businesses, every single day.

After hours

A client messages at 8pm wanting to book. You see it at 8am, if you see it at all — by then they've messaged someone else.

Mid-service

You're with a client, hands full or under a bonnet. The phone buzzes. It has to wait, and waiting is how bookings slip away.

Manual admin

Every booking typed by hand into a diary or spreadsheet is a chance to double-book, mishear a time, or lose the message entirely.

How Frontdesk answers

Same WhatsApp number your customers already have. Nothing for them to download.

  1. Client messages your WhatsApp number

    Any time — during business hours, after close, or on a Sunday.

  2. Frontdesk reads what they need

    Type of appointment, preferred day, urgency — understood from plain language, no menus.

  3. It checks your live diary

    Real open slots only — no double-bookings, no guessing.

  4. It confirms and books it

    The appointment lands on your calendar, confirmed with the client, before you've even opened the chat.

  5. You see it, already done

    A booked appointment, not a message you still owe someone a reply to.
