About Rivance

We build the boring part so you don't have to

Rivance exists because most South African SMEs lose more time and money to admin than to anything competitive happening in their market.

Why Rivance exists

Rivance was started by a small team of engineers based in South Africa who kept seeing the same pattern: a well-run business — a dentist, a salon, a workshop — bleeding revenue not because the work was bad, but because the admin around it was manual. Bookings typed by hand. Messages answered a day late. Reminders that never got sent.

None of that needed more software in the abstract sense. It needed the specific, unglamorous gap in each business closed — the phone that doesn't get answered, the diary that doesn't update itself, the report that takes a week to pull together. That's the work Rivance does.

What we do

Frontdesk — productised

A WhatsApp AI receptionist built specifically for appointment-based businesses. It's the one problem — missed bookings — that showed up in almost every SME we spoke to, so we built it once, properly, and made it available to any business that needs it.

Custom automation — bespoke

Beyond Frontdesk, Rivance builds and connects systems for individual businesses: linking invoicing to stock, automating management reports, wiring one piece of software to another so information stops being re-typed by hand. This work is scoped per business, not sold off a shelf.

How we work

Look at what you actually run

Your calendar, your invoicing, your booking system — not a generic checklist.

Find the specific bottleneck

The one manual step that's actually costing you time or bookings, not every possible improvement.

Build and connect it

Using the tools you already have where possible, not replacing them for the sake of it.

Support it ongoing

From South Africa, in your hours — automation you can reach a person about.

Have an operational bottleneck?

If it's appointment bookings, that's Frontdesk. If it's something else, tell us what's costing you time.
